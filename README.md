### 👑 About me

I'm **itz_raynx** — the **Founder & Owner of Team Nokiatis**.

We're **not just Minecraft launcher developers** — **my team and I build anything**.
What started as one launcher is now a full crew shipping desktop apps, modern websites,
Discord bots and Minecraft tooling — all engineered together, from idea to release.

- 👑 **Founder & Owner** — I lead the team and every project end to end
- 🌐 Our crew is growing — now taking on **web development** and beyond
- 🤝 Every line of code is a **team effort** — planned, built and shipped together
